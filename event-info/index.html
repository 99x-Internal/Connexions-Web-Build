What is included in a pass? +

Full access to all stages and studios, lunch, refreshments through the day, and entry to the rooftop afterparty.

Will sessions be recorded? +

Main Stage keynotes and panels are recorded and released to pass holders two weeks after the event. Studio sessions are off the record.

Yes. Passes can be transferred to a colleague up to 48 hours before doors open, free of charge.
