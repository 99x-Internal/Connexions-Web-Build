A quick introduction

Beyond the hype. Into the real world.

AI is no longer just something organisations are talking about. It is being built, tested, deployed and integrated into the way people work. But the reality is rarely as simple as the headlines suggest.

What happens when an AI initiative moves from an idea to implementation? What works? What doesn't? Where do organisations see real value, and where do they run into challenges they didn't expect?

Connexions Oslo brings together the people experiencing this first-hand to share what they have tried, what they have learned and what they would do differently.

What to expect

Real cases. Real lessons. Real conversations.

  • AI in the real world

    Forget the hypothetical. Hear how organisations are actually using AI, the problems they set out to solve, what happened when they implemented it and the results they have seen.

  • From AI experiments to AI agents

    AI agents are moving from conversation to action. Explore real use cases, early experiences and what organisations are learning as agents become part of software development and everyday workflows.

  • The foundations behind AI

    Behind every successful AI implementation are decisions around data, architecture, security and sovereignty. Hear how organisations are approaching these challenges in practice and what they have learned.

  • Does the value add up?

    AI investment comes with big expectations. But where is the actual return? Explore real experiences around cost, efficiency, productivity, investment and the challenges of turning AI potential into measurable business value.
